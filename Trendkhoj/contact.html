Frequently Asked Questions

Where does TrendKhoj India get its initial data?
Initially, TrendKhoj India displays an authentic, clearly labeled Demo Dataset representing realistic Indian search trends. It also empowers you to import any CSV or RSS feed directly.
Why does direct RSS URL fetching sometimes fail?
Web browsers enforce CORS (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing) security. External servers often block direct browser XML reading. That is why we provide a 100% reliable Safe Manual XML Paste tab!
Can I use the generated article outlines commercially?
Yes! The outline templates and headlines are free to adapt, edit, and publish in your newsletters, blogs, and news stories. Just ensure you independently verify all factual claims.
Does TrendKhoj track my searches or favorites?
Never. Your search history, favorites, custom datasets, and outline notes are stored 100% privately on your device via localStorage.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

At TrendKhoj India, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Because TrendKhoj India is built as a static client-side web application, our privacy policy is straightforward: we do not collect, monetize, or track your personal data.

1. Local Storage Usage (Private to Your Device)

TrendKhoj India uses your web browser's standard localStorage API to preserve your user preferences between visits. The following items are stored exclusively on your device:

  • Saved Topics (Favorites): The list of topics you star for subsequent review.
  • Recent Search Queries: The last 10 search queries you typed into the search bar, with a 1-click option to clear them at any time.
  • Custom Imported Datasets: Any CSV file or RSS feed you choose to import stays in your local browser memory.
  • Saved Article Outlines: Any customized notes you write and save in the Topic Explorer.
  • View Mode: Whether you prefer Table view or Card view.

None of these records are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. No Cookies or Third-Party Tracking

TrendKhoj India sets zero tracking cookies, zero profiling cookies, and zero fingerprinting scripts. We do not use third-party user analytics tracking or surveillance beacons.

3. Advertising Policy

We do not display deceptive ads, unclosable popups, or aggressive auto-redirects. Our code includes a cleanly labeled, non-intrusive container placeholder for approved advertising networks (such as Google AdSense), which will only operate subject to industry-standard consent banners if activated in the future.

4. External Links

Our platform links to external websites, including Google Trends, government portals (e.g., UPSC, PIB), sports bodies (BCCI), and media outlets. When navigating to these external sites, please review their respective privacy practices.

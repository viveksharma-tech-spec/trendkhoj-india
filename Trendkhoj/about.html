Our Mission & Methodology

About TrendKhoj India

TrendKhoj India was created to solve a straightforward challenge: making India's fast-moving public search trends and news conversations discoverable, well-categorized, and actionable for journalists, content creators, researchers, and everyday citizens.

Our Core Principles

  • Original Identity: Inspired by the intuitive clarity of Google Trends Trending Now, TrendKhoj India features custom branding, independent engineering, and India-specific categories like Cricket, Education, Sarkari Jobs, Automobile, and Government schemes.
  • Strict Data Authenticity: We never fabricate fake search numbers, rankings, or timestamps. If a dataset does not provide search volume, we explicitly state "Not Reported" rather than misleading our readers.
  • Transparent Demo Labeling: Any sample or demonstration data is visibly and prominently marked as a demo dataset. We never mislead users into believing illustrative data is a real-time live feed.
  • Zero Paid API Lock-In: Built entirely with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern client-side JavaScript. Everything runs statically on Netlify with zero paid cloud subscriptions.

The Golden Rule: Search Trends Are Not Verified Facts

⚠️
Editorial & Public Warning: Search trends merely measure public curiosity and query velocity on search engines. High search volume for an individual or claim is never proof of wrongdoing, innocence, truth, or factuality. Always verify through official press releases, government notifications, or established news desks before publishing.

Editorial Tools (Hindi & English)

Every topic in TrendKhoj India includes a built-in Topic Explorer equipped with client-side headline suggestions, social media thread starters, and customizable article outlines in English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Hinglish. These outlines use algorithmic templates and require zero external generative AI APIs or paid tokens.

Local Privacy

Your saved topics (Favorites), search history, custom imported CSVs, and edited article outlines are stored exclusively in your browser's private localStorage. No personal data, tracking cookies, or search queries are transmitted to external servers.
